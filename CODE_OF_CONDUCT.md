# Code of conduct

Kumi is built by and for people who run and play at padel clubs, in English and Spanish. Everyone
taking part (in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space for the project) is
expected to:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project, to programming or to English.
- Disagree about the work, never attack the person. Assume good intent and ask before concluding.
- Keep it on topic, and keep private information private: members' names, contact details and
  anything a club shares stays out of public threads.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, unwelcome sexual attention, discriminatory jokes or language,
threats, publishing someone's private information, and deliberately disrupting the project.

**Reporting.** Tell the maintainers privately (the contact is listed here before the project accepts
outside contributions). Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, code or
issues that break this code, and may ban anyone who does so repeatedly or seriously.
